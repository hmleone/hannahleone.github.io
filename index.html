Hi, I am

Hannah

Graphic Designer | Web Design Student | Creative Thinker

Hi! I’m a graphic designer with a love for all things creative and a focus on crafting designs that not only look good but work well. I graduated from the University at Buffalo with a degree in Graphic Design and a minor in Communications, and recently made the move from upstate New York to Phoenix. Over the last three years, I’ve been diving into the design world here in Arizona, building my portfolio and connecting with other creatives. Right now, I’m pursuing web design to broaden my skill set. I’m excited to combine my background in graphic using with new web technologies, focusing on creating user-friendly, functional websites that still pack a visual punch. Collaboration is key for me, I thrive in environments that are supportive, positive, and where teamwork is at forefront. I’m always passionate about bringing fresh ideas to life through design. Thank you so much for taking the time to visit my site and explore my portfolio.
